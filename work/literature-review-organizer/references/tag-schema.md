# Tag Schema

Use the user's labels when supplied. Otherwise generate candidate labels under these dimensions:

## Topic

Research subjects, processes, pathways, diseases, materials, technologies, or theories.

## Method

Experimental, computational, statistical, imaging, omics, modeling, review, meta-analysis, or validation methods.

## Object

Species, cell type, tissue, material, population, dataset, model system, or study unit.

## Phenotype or outcome

Measured traits, endpoints, performance indicators, clinical outcomes, molecular outcomes, or predicted outcomes.

## Environment or condition

Location, time, treatment, stress, dose, temperature, developmental stage, intervention, or other context.

## Reading status

- 待阅读
- 泛读
- 精读
- 已提取数据

## Research role

- 背景
- 方法
- 证据
- 候选机制
- 争议
- 局限
- 创新点
- 待验证

Use a consistent naming convention within one project. Keep topic labels separate from reading-status labels.
